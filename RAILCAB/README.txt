RAILCAB - an original train-driving game for the HP Prime
=========================================================
Drive a passenger train from the cab. Obey the signals and speed
limits, stop at the STOP marker, open the doors, let everyone board,
close the doors and keep to the timetable.

THE DASHBOARD
  Bottom-left panel : train class, unit number, headcode, route,
                      next stop, timetable time and delay
                      (green = on time or early, red = late),
                      distance to the stop and the platform number
  Signal box        : number of the next signal, its distance and
                      its aspect (red / yellow / green)
  Clock             : current time
  Next limit box    : the next speed limit and how far away it is
  Power/brake bar   : green = power, red = brake
  Speed dial        : WHITE needle = your speed,
                      GREEN marker = the max speed you have set.
                      The train speeds up or brakes on its own to
                      reach the green marker.
  Red disc          : the current speed limit (it flashes when you
                      are too fast)

HOW TO DRIVE
  1. At the start the doors are open. When everyone has boarded,
     press DOOR (or Enter) to close them. The signal turns green.
  2. Raise the green marker to set your max speed. Stay below the
     red speed-limit disc.
  3. A yellow signal means the next one is red. Slow down and stop
     before any red signal. It clears after a while.
  4. Bring the green marker down early. Stop with the cab at the
     white STOP marker at the end of the platform. "BRAKE!" flashes
     if you are coming in too fast.
  5. When stopped at the platform, press DOOR to open the doors.
     Close them again when everyone has boarded.

SCORING
  Stop within 1.5 m of the marker : Perfect stop +150
  Within 5 m / 12 m               : +100 / +50
  On time (up to 60 s early or 30 s late)  : +100
  Overspeed: -10 every second. Far too fast trips the emergency brake (-100)
  Passing a red signal (SPAD): -400   Missing a stop: -300
  Hitting the buffers: -500. Your best score per route is saved.

ROUTES (all fictional)
  Saltmere Coast Line  - sunny seaside, beaches, a long estuary bridge
  Pinewood Valley Line - pine forests, rain, tunnels and river bridges
                         (use the wipers!)
  Dusk Metro           - city at sunset, brick walls, lit tower blocks

TRAINS
  Class 271 Gull     - 75 mph commuter train, quick to accelerate
  Class 418 Kestrel  - 110 mph intercity train, heavy and long
  Class 156 Badger   - 60 mph 2-car regional train

TOUCH CONTROLS
  Tap or drag on the speed dial to set the green marker.
  SET v / SET ^  : marker -5 / +5 mph
  DOOR : open/close doors     STOP : emergency brake
  WIPE : wipers on/off        MAP  : route map
KEYBOARD
  Up / + : marker +5      Down / - : marker -5
  Right  : marker = speed limit     Left : marker 0
  Enter  : doors          Del : emergency brake
  Menu   : route map      Space : wipers
  7 / 9  : shorter / longer view distance       Esc : pause / quit

INSTALL
  Copy the RAILCAB.hpappdir folder to the calculator with HP
  Connectivity Kit, then open RAILCAB from the Apps screen.

STREETZ - a low-poly open-world city game for the HP Prime
==========================================================
An original game in the style of open-world crime games. Walk around a
3D city, take any car, drive through traffic, do jobs for cash and shake
off the police.

THE CITY
  8x8 blocks of buildings (taller downtown), parks, traffic that drives
  in lanes and turns at intersections, police patrols, parked cars.

JOBS (yellow marker - walk or drive into it)
  DELIVERY - drive to the green marker before the timer runs out.
  GETAWAY  - you start with 2 stars; reach the green marker AND lose
             the cops to get paid.
  A green arrow at the top points to the drop-off.

WANTED STARS (max 3)
  Taking a car from traffic while a cop is around (or by bad luck),
  stealing a police car, ramming cops or reckless driving near them.
  Lose them: stay out of police sight for ~9 seconds.
  Or drive into the PINK respray shop ($50) to clear your stars.
  Stop next to a cop for a few seconds = BUSTED (-$100).
  Your cash is saved.

TOUCH CONTROLS (on screen)
  < >          steer (in a car) / turn (on foot)
  GAS (up)     drive / walk     BRAKE (down)  brake, reverse, walk back
  CAR / EXIT   get in the nearest car / get out

KEYBOARD
  Up/8 gas  Down/5 brake  Left/4 Right/6 steer   Enter: get in/out
  Menu: radar on/off   Del: touch buttons on/off
  + / -: view distance (lower = faster)   Esc: save and quit

INSTALL (same as the CubeGame example)
  Keep the folder name "STREETZ.hpappdir" exactly as it is.
  1. Start the HP Prime Virtual Calculator or connect your HP Prime.
  2. Open HP Connectivity Kit.
  3. Drag "STREETZ.hpappdir" onto the calculator's "Application Library".
  4. On the calculator press Apps, tap "STREETZ".
  Requires firmware with Python support (2020 or newer).
  This is the heaviest of the games; on a G1 press "-" for speed.

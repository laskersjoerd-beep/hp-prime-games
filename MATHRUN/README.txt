MATHRUN - an original school-maze math game for the HP Prime
============================================================
You're stuck in school after hours. Find the 7 notebooks hidden in the
classrooms, solve their math problems, and escape through a green EXIT
before Ms. Margin - the strictest grader in school - catches you.

HOW IT WORKS
  - Each notebook asks 3 math problems. Type the answer with the
    calculator's number keys (or tap the keypad) and press Enter / OK.
    Problems get harder as you go (adding -> times tables -> mixed).
  - Your first notebook wakes Ms. Margin up.
  - Every WRONG answer makes her angrier and faster, and she knows
    where you are for a while. All 3 right = she doesn't notice you.
  - The red pulsing border means she is close.
  - With all 7 notebooks, the EXIT signs light up green - run!

ALSO IN THE SCHOOL
  Mop Bot       - leaves wet floors: you slide and can't stop quickly
  Paper-plane kid - throws paper planes that daze you for a moment
  Items (3 slots): Energy Bar (full stamina), Eraser (throw it when
  she is close: she can't see for 5 s), Sticky Map (shows notebooks)
  Every game has a new school layout.

TOUCH CONTROLS
  ^ v < > walk/turn, RUN (uses stamina), USE item, SWAP item slot
KEYBOARD
  Arrows walk/turn, Enter run, Space use item, + swap slot,
  Menu map on/off, Del hide buttons, 7 / 9 less / more detail
  (80, 107, 160 or 320 columns - 160 is the default), Esc quit

GRAPHICS
  Walls use detailed 64x64 pixel textures (plaster, notice boards,
  lockers, wooden doors, EXIT doors) drawn in thin 2-pixel columns,
  and every character is hand-drawn in detail with animation:
  Ms. Margin walks (and turns red when angry), the Mop Bot's mop spins
  and the kid winds up before throwing a paper plane.

INSTALL
  Keep the folder name "MATHRUN.hpappdir", drag it onto the calculator's
  Application Library in HP Connectivity Kit, then Apps > MATHRUN.

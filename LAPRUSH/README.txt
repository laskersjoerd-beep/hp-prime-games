LAPRUSH 2 - low-poly stunt racer for the HP Prime
=================================================
Time-trial racing in the style of low-poly stunt racers. Version 2 adds
full 3D track physics: loops, wall rides, magnetic ceilings, jumps.

TRACKS (8)
  1 MEADOW       classic, 3 laps, grass slows you down
  2 SKYWAY       classic, 3 laps, floating track - don't fall off
  3 LOOP VALLEY  jump over a gap, a full loop, a crest that sends you flying
  4 WALL RIDER   two big banked wall rides (~75 degrees) and a jump
  5 UPSIDE DOWN  corkscrew onto a magnetic ceiling, loop, jump, wall turn
  6 SPEEDWAY     2.7 km  - long sweeping bends, boost strips, a big jump
  7 GRAND TOUR   2.9 km  - everything: boosts, 2 loops, wall ride, magnetic
                 ceiling, jumps, banked turns
  8 MARATHON     4.0 km  - endless hills and S-bends with boost jumps
  Tracks 3-8 are point-to-point: start -> checkpoints -> finish.

SPEED
  Top speed on gas alone is ~187 km/h (classic tracks 1-2: ~158 km/h).
  GREEN BOOST STRIPS with white arrows shoot you up to ~360 km/h; the
  extra speed fades away slowly. At high speed the view widens, speed
  lines streak past and the speedometer turns yellow, then red.
  The track is drawn much further ahead so you can see what's coming.

MENU
  A live fly-through of the selected track plays in the background.
  The card shows the track name, length, features (BOOST, LOOP, WALL,
  MAGNET, JUMP, LAPS) and your record. Tap the arrows or press
  Left/Right (or 1-8) to pick, tap RACE! or press Enter to go.

PHYSICS
  - The car sticks to the road while it is pressed into it (speed through
    the curve + gravity). Too slow in a loop or on a wall = you fall off.
  - PURPLE road is magnetic: it holds you even upside down.
  - Ramps and crests launch you into the air; you can steer a little in
    the air and land on the track further on. Miss it and you reset.
  - Climbing slows you down, going downhill speeds you up.
  - The track never turns your car - not in bends, banks or loops. Your
    car keeps pointing where you point it, so steer (and brake) yourself.
    Loops twist sideways (the exit is beside the entry): the loop carries
    you across, but your car keeps facing the same way.
  - DRIFTING: at speed, hold BRAKE while steering to kick the rear out.
    Then switch to GAS and counter-steer to hold the slide, or let go
    of the steering to straighten up. Drifting turns you much tighter
    than grip, but sliding sideways costs a bit of speed. Tyre smoke and
    drift points show how well you drift (points are on the results).
  - Want to brake WITHOUT drifting? Brake before the corner, then steer.
  - Tip: hold GAS through loops and wall rides.

TOUCH CONTROLS (multi-touch: hold GAS and steer at once)
  [<] [>] steer   BRAKE   GAS   RESET (top) = back to last checkpoint

KEYBOARD
  Up/8 gas   Down/5 brake   Left/4 Right/6 steer   Enter: reset
  Menu: minimap   Del: hide touch buttons   + / -: draw distance
  Esc: back to menu.

INSTALL
  Keep the folder name "LAPRUSH.hpappdir" exactly as it is.
  Drag "LAPRUSH.hpappdir" onto the calculator's "Application Library" in
  HP Connectivity Kit (it replaces the old LAPRUSH; records are kept),
  then press Apps and tap "LAPRUSH". Firmware with Python (2020+).
  If it runs slowly, press "-" to lower the draw distance.

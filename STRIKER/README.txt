STRIKER - an original tanks-and-planes battle game for the HP Prime
===================================================================
Two teams, Blue (you) and Red, each have 3 tanks and 2 fighter-bombers.
All the other vehicles are bots. They fight over capture point A in the
middle of the map. Destroying a vehicle costs its team 1 ticket. Holding
point A drains 1 enemy ticket every 6 seconds. The team that runs out of
tickets (25 each) loses. Bots respawn after 15 s, and you can pick a tank
or a plane every time you respawn.

YOUR VEHICLES
  Bastion  - tank. Strong front armour, weaker sides, weakest rear.
             Main gun (reload 4 s) plus a machine gun that can aim at planes.
             Only tanks can capture point A (drive into the circle).
  Swift    - fighter-bomber. Two wing guns and 2 bombs.
             Watch your speed: below about 140 km/h it stalls.

THE DASHBOARD (all icons)
  Arrows      : drive / fly
  Shield bar  : health
  Shell + bar : main gun reload (green = ready) and shells left
  Bullets     : machine-gun / wing-gun ammo
  Bomb        : bombs left (plane)
  Green digits: speed in km/h.  Plane: blue digits = height above the
                ground, orange bar = throttle (tap its top/bottom half)
  Big red button : FIRE (main gun / wing guns)
  Bullets / bomb button : machine gun (tank) / drop bomb (plane)
  Crosshair   : gunner sight zoom (tank) / close camera (plane)
  Map         : battle map
  Top bar     : Blue tickets | point A (colour = owner, bar = capture) |
                Red tickets.  Red markers = enemies, blue = allies.

CONTROLS
  Tank : Up/Down drive, Left/Right turn the hull.
         Aim the turret with 4/6 (left/right) and 8/2 (up/down), or drag
         on the 3D view. Enter = fire, Del = machine gun, 0 = sight zoom.
  Plane: Up = climb, Down = dive, Left/Right = bank and turn, or drag on
         the 3D view like a joystick. + / - = throttle.
         Enter = guns, Del = bomb, 0 = camera.
         The small red ring is where to aim to hit the nearest enemy plane.
  Menu : map     7 / 9 : shorter / longer view distance     Esc : pause

MAPS: Green Valley (fields, lakes and a village)
      Dust Ridge (desert hills)
BOT SKILL: Easy / Normal / Hard (how well the bots aim)

INSTALL
  Copy the STRIKER.hpappdir folder to the calculator with HP Connectivity
  Kit, then open STRIKER from the Apps screen.

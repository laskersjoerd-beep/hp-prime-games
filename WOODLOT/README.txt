WOODLOT - a lumber tycoon game for the HP Prime
===============================================
An original island where you chop trees, haul logs, saw planks, sell
wood and build your own house. Your money, gear and buildings are saved.

THE ISLAND
  Millbrook Town (start) - Axe Barn, Truck Yard, Saw Works and the green
  SELL pad.  Your Plot is just east of town (yellow on the map).
  Forests: Pine Forest (NW), Old Oak Wood (W), The Bog (SW),
  Birch Meadow (S), Frost Hills (N, snowy) and Cinder Isle (far east,
  across the bridge, with a volcano).

WOOD (better wood is worth more but is harder to chop)
  Pine $8/m3, Birch $12, Oak $18, Frost Pine $26 (needs Iron Axe),
  Bogwood $42 (needs Iron Axe), Emberwood $110 (needs Steel Axe).
  Bigger, longer trees give bigger logs = more money.

HOW TO PLAY
  1. Walk to a tree and hold ACTION (CHOP) until it falls.
  2. ACTION (GRAB) on the log to drag it, ACTION again to DROP it.
  3. Drop logs on the green SELL pad in town for money...
  4. ...or drop them in YOUR SAWMILL on your plot: it cuts them into
     planks, which sell for 60% more.
  5. Buy better axes (Axe Barn), a pickup truck that carries 4 logs
     (Truck Yard) and faster sawmills (Saw Works).
     Drop logs next to your truck to load them, GRAB them to unload.
  6. BUILD (on your plot): place floor / wall / post blueprints on the
     grid (2 levels, ROT turns walls). Then carry planks to a blueprint
     and DROP them on it - it turns into real wood of that type!
  Chopped trees grow back after a while.

TOUCH CONTROLS
  < > turn   ^ v walk / drive   ACTION (changes: CHOP, GRAB, DROP, SHOP,
  DRIVE, EXIT)   BUILD (only on your plot)
KEYBOARD
  Arrows / 8456 move, Enter or Space = ACTION (hold to keep chopping),
  Menu = build (on your plot) / map, Del = hide buttons, Esc = save+quit

INSTALL (same as the other games)
  Keep the folder name "WOODLOT.hpappdir", drag it onto the calculator's
  Application Library in HP Connectivity Kit, then Apps > WOODLOT.

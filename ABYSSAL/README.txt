ABYSSAL - deep-sea facility horror for the HP Prime
===================================================
An original game inspired by room-by-room deep-sea horror games.
Descend through 40 randomly generated rooms of a flooded research
facility and reach the escape pod. Every dive is a new layout.

DANGERS
  SURGE   - When the lights flicker, something is coming. Get into a
            LOCKER before it rushes through, or it gets you.
  ECHO    - (deeper rooms) Same warning, but it turns around and comes
            back 2-3 times. Stay hidden until "It's gone".
  WATCHER - A floating eye. Do NOT look straight at it - turn away.
  PRESSURE- Don't stay in a locker too long (watch the meter).

ITEMS
  Keycard - every 6th door is locked (red light); its keycard is in
            the same room. Medkits restore health.
  Doors open automatically. The last room has the escape pod hatch
  (blinking cyan) - face it and press ACT.

TOUCH CONTROLS (on screen; Del hides/shows them)
  ^ v < >  bottom-left   forward / back / turn
  ACT      bottom-right  hide in locker / leave locker / use

KEYBOARD
  Up / 8  forward      Down / 5  back       Left / Right  turn
  4 / 6   strafe       Space or Enter  ACT  + / -  detail
  Del     touch buttons on/off             Esc  quit

INSTALL (same as the CubeGame example)
  Keep the folder name "ABYSSAL.hpappdir" exactly as it is.
  1. Start the HP Prime Virtual Calculator or connect your HP Prime.
  2. Open HP Connectivity Kit.
  3. Drag "ABYSSAL.hpappdir" onto the calculator's "Application Library".
  4. On the calculator press Apps, tap "ABYSSAL".
  Requires firmware with Python support (2020 or newer).
  If it runs slowly (G1), press "-" to lower the detail.

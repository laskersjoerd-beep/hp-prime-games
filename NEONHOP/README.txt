NEONHOP 2 - a one-button rhythm platformer for the HP Prime
===========================================================
Tap ANYWHERE on the screen to play (or Up / Space / Enter / 8 / +).
Esc = back to the menu (Esc on the menu quits).

ALL 8 GAME MODES
  CUBE    tap/hold to jump
  SHIP    hold to fly up, release to drop
  BALL    tap to flip gravity (roll on the floor or the ceiling)
  UFO     every tap is a little jump in the air
  WAVE    hold = go up diagonally, release = go down diagonally
  ROBOT   hold longer = jump higher
  SPIDER  tap to teleport straight to the ceiling / floor
  SWING   tap to flip which way you fall while flying

OBJECTS
  White spikes      touching one = crash
  Neon blocks       land on them (ship/ball/ufo/wave/swing can also
                    slide under them); hitting the side = crash
  Yellow pad        launches you automatically
  Yellow orb        press while touching it = jump again in mid-air
                    (works with a tap OR if you are still holding)
  Blue orb          press while touching it = flip gravity
  Coloured portals  change mode (letter on top: C S B U W R P G)
  u / d portals     gravity upside down / normal
  > / < portals     fast speed / normal speed

LEVELS (all verified beatable)
  1 FIRST HOP  2 NEON DRIFT  3 OVERCLOCK          (the originals)
  4 BOUNCE PARK    ball, UFO and upside-down cube
  5 WAVE RIDER     wave zig-zags and spider teleports
  6 ROBOT WORKS    robot high jumps and a swing tunnel
  7 FULL SPECTRUM  every mode, gravity and speed portals
  8 / 9 MY LEVEL 1 / 2  your own levels from the editor

LOOKS
  Every mode has its own detailed, animated design: a visor cube, a
  jet with afterburner, a spinning ball, a saucer with blinking
  lights, a glowing dart, a stepping robot, a scuttling spider and a
  rotor pod. They flip properly when gravity is upside down.

EFFECTS
  Particle trails for every mode (flames behind the ship and swing),
  a flash + big mode name when you go through a portal, rings and
  sparks when you hit an orb or pad, landing dust, a wave trail,
  pulsing orbs, shimmering portals, blocks that flash on the beat,
  parallax background, screen shake when you crash and confetti when
  you finish. The menu is animated too.

LEVEL EDITOR  (menu: 0. LEVEL EDITOR, pick a slot)
  Tap a tile in the palette at the bottom, then tap the grid to place
  it. Tap the same tile again to erase it. Portals fill their column.
  MOVING AROUND:
    - DRAG the grid left/right with your finger to scroll
    - the thin bar under the buttons is a map of the WHOLE level:
      tap or drag on it to jump anywhere (yellow box = what you see)
    - << / >> jump 12 columns, arrow keys move the cursor (hold to
      keep moving; the view follows), 7 / 9 also scroll
  PLAY tests the level, SAVE saves, EXIT saves + quits.
  Keys: arrows move the cursor, Enter place, Del erase, +/- pick tile,
        1 play, Menu save, Esc exit.
  Levels are saved on the calculator as nhlevel1.txt / nhlevel2.txt
  and show up in the menu as MY LEVEL 1 / 2.

INSTALL
  Keep the folder name "NEONHOP.hpappdir" exactly as it is and drag it
  onto the calculator's "Application Library" in HP Connectivity Kit
  (replaces the old NEONHOP; your best % are kept). Firmware with
  Python (2020+).
